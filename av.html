What I do

From planning and deployment through to live operation.

System Planning

Designing audio, video and lighting systems that fit your venue, event or production. I think about signal flow, cable routes, equipment requirements and future scaling.

Audio Management

Professional audio engineering for live events, recording sessions, streaming and presentations. Microphone placement, mixing, monitoring and troubleshooting.

Lighting Operation

Live lighting control for gigs, events and shows. I work with both automated and manual systems, creating atmosphere and emphasizing your production's key moments.

Show Operation

Day-of technical direction and problem-solving. I coordinate audio, lighting and video to ensure your event runs smoothly from soundcheck through to pack-down.

Video & Streaming

Multi-camera video capture, switching and live streaming setup. From basic single-feed streaming to complex multi-platform production.

Equipment Hire & Setup

I supply, install and configure professional AV equipment for your event. Everything from microphones and speakers to lighting rigs and video switching.
