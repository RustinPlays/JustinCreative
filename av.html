What I do

From planning and deployment through to live operation.

System Planning

Designing audio, video and lighting systems that fit your venue, event or production. I think about signal flow, cable routes, equipment requirements and future scaling.

Audio Management

Professional audio engineering for live events, recording sessions, streaming and presentations. Microphone placement, mixing, monitoring and troubleshooting.

Lighting Operation

Live lighting control for gigs, events and shows. I work with both automated and manual systems, creating atmosphere and emphasizing your production's key moments.

Show Operation

Day-of technical direction and problem-solving. I coordinate audio, lighting and video to ensure your event runs smoothly from soundcheck through to pack-down.

Video & Streaming

I set up video switching and live streaming around your event's needs, using suitable venue equipment or arranging hire through external AV companies.

Equipment Hire & Setup

I own the lighting equipment I offer for hire. For microphones, speakers, video switching or extra lighting, I can arrange equipment through external AV companies, then install and configure the setup for your event.

Good to know

The practical details, agreed before show day.

What equipment, access and power do you need?

Send me the venue details, run sheet and any existing equipment list. I will confirm the system, safe access, power, setup time and crew needed, including what I supply and what the venue or another supplier provides.

Can you work alongside another production company?

Yes. Tell me who is supplying and operating each part of the event. I can work within the wider production team, with responsibilities, signal connections and the run sheet agreed in advance.

Do you travel outside Auckland?

Share the location and event times with your enquiry. I will confirm availability and include any travel, accommodation or transport costs in the quote.

What happens if the event runs overtime?

We agree the booked hours and any overtime terms before the event. If timings change on the day, speak to me as early as possible so we can confirm whether I can stay and any extra cost before extending the booking.

Can you use the venue's equipment?

Send through its equipment list and a technical contact. I will check suitability and compatibility, then identify any extra hire or setup needed in the quote.
