Good to know

A few details to help plan your shoot.

How long does editing take?

Turnaround depends on the shoot and number of images. I will confirm a delivery date with your quote. If you need images for an announcement or a particular deadline, include that in your enquiry.

How will I receive my photographs?

I supply edited digital images. We will agree the online gallery or download method, image selection and file sizes before the shoot, including any versions you need for print or social media.

Are revisions or extra retouching included?

I will set out the editing scope and any included revision rounds in your quote. Let me know about specific retouching requirements before booking; extra edits can be discussed and quoted separately.

Do you supply RAW files?

The usual deliverable is the finished, edited photographs. If you need RAW files or unedited images, mention this before booking so we can agree whether they can be supplied and on what terms.

Where can I use the photographs?

Tell me how you plan to use them, such as personal prints, social media, a website or advertising. I will confirm the included usage and licensing in the quote. If another business needs to use the images, include that too.

Can you travel, and what if the shoot runs late?

Send me the location and schedule. I will confirm availability, any travel costs, the booked hours and overtime terms in the quote. If you need longer on the day, we will agree any extension and cost before continuing.
