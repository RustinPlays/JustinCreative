Installed or operated.

Both options include delivery, setup, programming, testing and packdown.

01

Auto mode

I install and test the system, then leave it ready to run automatically or react to sound.

02

Live operated

I stay for up to five event hours, creating live looks, movement and colour changes throughout the event.

Good to know

I’ll confirm the final setup requirements with your quote.

Can I book lighting on its own?

Yes. Every package can be hired independently for parties, weddings, venues and other events.

Can haze be used everywhere?

No. I only use haze where the venue permits it and its alarm requirements allow it. The packages still work without haze.

What access and power do you need?

I’ll need safe setup access, suitable power and enough room for the selected stands or truss. Send me the venue details and I’ll confirm what is required.

Can you create a custom package?

Yes. If the standard packages do not suit the room, guest count or atmosphere, I can recommend a custom setup.
