The Process

Planning

We develop a creative brief, shot list and timeline. I consider the story, pacing and how we'll tell it visually.

Production

Professional shoot with cinema-quality equipment. Multi-camera when needed, with attention to light, sound and composition.

Post-Production

Colour grading, sound design, motion graphics and effects. Every frame polished and purposeful.

Delivery

Final files in your required format. From short-form social content to feature-length projects, I deliver it ready to go.

Good to know

From the first cut to the final files.

When will I receive the first cut and final video?

The timeline depends on the shoot, edit length and feedback needed. I will agree first-cut and final-delivery dates with you before work begins. Tell me about any launch or event deadline in your enquiry.

How many revisions are included?

The number of revision rounds is agreed in your quote. Sending one consolidated set of feedback per round helps keep the edit on schedule. Additional rounds or changes to the original brief can be quoted before I make them.

How are the finished files delivered?

We will agree the download method, resolution, aspect ratios and file formats for your intended platforms. Mention vertical social clips, captions or alternate versions in your brief so they can be included in the scope.

Can I have the raw footage or editing project?

Raw footage and editable project files need to be discussed before booking. I will confirm what can be supplied, any third-party asset restrictions and the delivery terms in the quote.

What usage and music licensing is included?

Tell me where the video will appear, including any paid advertising. I will confirm the intended usage and any music or stock-asset licensing in the quote. If you provide music or other material, we will need to check that it is cleared for the planned use.

Can you travel or work with another production team?

Include the location, schedule and other crew in your enquiry. I will confirm travel costs, equipment and responsibilities with the quote. We also agree the booked shoot hours and overtime terms, with any extension subject to availability and agreed cost.
